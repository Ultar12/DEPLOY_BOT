ABOUT ULTAR SYNC

Built around useful products, software, and dependable relationships.

Ultar Sync is a general merchandise and software services business based in Obeledu, Anambra, Nigeria. We support customers by sourcing and supplying everyday merchandise and by providing practical software and digital assistance according to their needs.

Our approach is simple: communicate clearly, treat customers respectfully, and provide practical assistance throughout each engagement. We are committed to building a trustworthy and accessible business for the communities we serve.
